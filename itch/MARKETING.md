## Trailer aufnehmen
1. `index.html` (Dev-Version) im Browser öffnen, Fenster schmal/hochkant machen.
2. Mac: Cmd + Shift + 5 → "Ausgewählten Bereich aufnehmen" → Spielfeld markieren → Aufnahme starten.
3. Im Menü oben links auf **TRAILER** klicken. Läuft ca. 34 Sekunden automatisch (Titel → Gameplay → 5 Bosse → Endkarte).
4. Aufnahme stoppen. Antippen bricht den Trailer ab.
Tipp: Ton an lassen, die Musik passt zum Clip.

## Erster itch.io-Devlog
**Titel:** Neon Dodge is out! 10 bosses, skins and a skill tree

Neon Dodge is now playable for free in your browser and on your phone!

**What's in the game**
- Endless waves that slowly get harder
- 10 unique bosses every 10 waves, each with its own attacks: lasers, mines, splitting bullets, a black hole and more
- Final boss OVERLORD at wave 100, then Endless mode
- Core orbs that stun the boss, golden orbs, shield, slow-mo and bomb power-ups
- Skill tree with permanent upgrades
- 12 skins to unlock with challenges
- Ranked ladder from Bronze to Legend, daily missions and streaks

**What's next**
I'm listening to your feedback! Tell me in the comments which boss is too hard (or too easy).

Thanks for playing! If you like it, a donation helps me keep making games.
