# Liminal VHS Shader Pack — Godot 4

**Make your game feel like a lost tape.**

The exact effects used in the liminal horror game *Neon Exile*, cleaned up into one drop-in node.

### Features
- 📼 **VHS screen filter** – scanlines, chroma bleed, tracking band, noise, vignette. One slider.
- 👻 **Liminal shell shader** – cold rim light, scanlines and a glitching double image for any model (works on animated characters).
- 💭 **Memory flashback** – white flash, warm tint, a whispered line of text.
- 🌞 **Hallucination** – for a few seconds the world snaps to "real" daylight and the monsters vanish. Then the tape comes back.
- ⚡ **Glitch bursts** for hits and scares.
- Walkable **demo scene** included.
- Godot **4.3+**, all renderers (Compatibility/Mobile/Forward+), pure GDScript + shaders, no plugins needed.

### How to use
Copy `addons/liminal_vhs` into your project, add a `LiminalFX` node, call `set_vhs()`, `flashback()`, `hallucinate()`. Full guide in the README.
