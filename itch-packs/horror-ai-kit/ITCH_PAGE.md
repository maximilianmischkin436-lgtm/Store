# Horror AI Kit — Godot 4

**Teachers who must not see you. Something running behind you. A hallway that never ends.**

Three horror setpieces from *Neon Exile*, as clean drop-in scripts.

### Included
- 🔦 **Watcher** – patrols with a visible light cone, line-of-sight + alert meter, instant fail when it sees you. Can't be killed. Optional "ignore the player" rule (ticket inspectors!).
- 🏃 **Chaser** – rubber-band pursuer that stays just behind you, twitchy and relentless, with a red glow and footsteps.
- 🚪 **Stretching Corridor** – the exit door runs away for N seconds while the chaser closes in.
- Default bodies included, or use your own model (`Visual` child node).
- Signals for everything (`spotted`, `caught`, `door_reached`) – hook up death screens, alarms, cutscenes.
- Playable **demo** (stealth room + chase corridor). Godot **4.3+**, all renderers, pure GDScript.
