# Liminal Sound Pack

Music, ambience loops and one-shot sounds for liminal, dreamcore and analog-horror games – from the game *Neon Exile*.

**Formats:** OGG (Godot, Unity, Unreal) + MP3 for music/ambience, OGG + WAV for SFX.

## Music (15 tracks, ~1.5–2 min, loopable)
Poolrooms_4PM · Neon_Mall_Muzak · Archive_Hum · After_School_Sunset · Ward_4 · Home_At_Six · Last_Train · Somewhere_Green · The_Crown · Golden_Afterworld · Childhood_Memory_Piano · Liminal_Dream · Empty_Exploration · Boss_Panic · Hallway_Chase

## Ambience loops (9)
Indoor_Pool_Echo · Abandoned_Mall · Fluorescent_Office · Empty_School_Hallway · Hospital_Corridor_Hum · Empty_Apartment_Night · Underground_Station · Meadow_Wind_Birds · White_Void_Drone

## SFX (8)
Distant_Child_Laugh · Phone_Ringing_Far · Footsteps_Behind_You · Broken_Music_Box · Distant_Door_Creak · PA_Chime_Announcement · Creature_Running · Dog_Bark

**Tip:** play the SFX rarely (every 30–60 s), quietly and *behind* the player with 3D positioning – that is what makes an empty place feel inhabited.

**Godot looping:** select the .ogg → Import tab → enable *Loop* → Reimport.
